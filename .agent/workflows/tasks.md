## 🎨 STYLE GUIDE

Tüm ikonlar için:
- **Pixel art style** (Minecraft-like but original)
- **Transparent background** (PNG alpha)
- **NO white background**, sadece ikon
- Centered icon, 70-80% canvas usage
- 1-2px dark outline
- Flat pixel shading (no gradients, no blur)
- Clean silhouette, readable at small size
